## Initial troubleshooting

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can do the initial troubleshooting of a IBM Storage Ceph cluster before contacting IBM Support.

Initial troubleshooting requires a running IBM Storage Ceph cluster.

For more information about contacting IBM Support, see ../navigation/contact_ibm_support.html.
