## Recover with one-way mirroring

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Learn how to recover from a disaster with one-way mirroring.

To recover from a disaster when using one-way mirroring use the following procedures. They show
how to fail over to the secondary cluster after the primary cluster terminates, and how to fail
back. The shutdown can be orderly or non-orderly.

> 📝 **Note:** Important:
One-way mirroring supports multiple secondary sites. If you are using
additional secondary clusters, choose one of the secondary clusters to fail over to. Synchronize
from the same cluster during fail back.
