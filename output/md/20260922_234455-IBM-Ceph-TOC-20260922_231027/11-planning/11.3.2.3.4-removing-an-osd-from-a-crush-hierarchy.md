## Removing an OSD from a CRUSH Hierarchy

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Removing an OSD from a CRUSH hierarchy is the first step when you want to remove an OSD
from your cluster. When you remove the OSD from the CRUSH map, CRUSH recomputes which OSDs get the
placement groups and data re-balances accordingly.

To remove an OSD from the CRUSH map of a running cluster, run the following:

Syntax

```plaintext-ibm
ceph osd crush remove NAME
```
