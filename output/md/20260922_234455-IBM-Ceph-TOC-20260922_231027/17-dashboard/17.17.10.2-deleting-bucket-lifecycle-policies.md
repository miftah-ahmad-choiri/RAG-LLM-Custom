## Deleting bucket lifecycle policies

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Use the dashboard to delete Ceph Object Gateway bucket lifecycle policies on the
IBM Storage Ceph dashboard.

### Procedure

1. From the dashboard, go to
Object
>
Buckets
.
2. Select the bucket for which the lifecycle policy needs to be deleted, and click
Edit
.
3. In the
Edit Bucket
form, go to
Policies
.
4. Click
Clear
.
5. To complete the bucket policy deletion, click
Edit
Bucket
.
