## Managing NFS shares

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can manage the NFS shares that use Ceph Object Gateway or CephFS as the backstore on the Ceph Dashboard. You can deploy and configure, edit and delete the NFS service daemons directly from the dashboard.

The dashboard manages NFS service configuration files that are stored in RADOS objects on the Ceph Cluster. NFS service must store part of their configuration in the Ceph cluster.

> 📝 **Note:** Note:
This documentation uses
*NFS share*
instead of the earlier term
*NFS export*
. In the Ceph Dashboard, labels and controls may still refer to NFS export.
