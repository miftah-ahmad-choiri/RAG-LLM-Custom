## Deleting a bucket notification

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Delete a bucket notification that is no longer required.

### Before you begin

Before you begin, make sure that you have the following prerequisites in place:

- The notification is not required by production services.

### Procedure

1. Go to the
Notifications
tab.
2. Select the bucket notification.
3. Click
Delete
.
4. Confirm the deletion.

### Results

The selected bucket notification is deleted..
