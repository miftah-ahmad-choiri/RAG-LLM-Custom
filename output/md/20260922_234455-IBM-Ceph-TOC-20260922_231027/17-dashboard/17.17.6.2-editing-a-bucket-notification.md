## Editing a bucket notification

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Modify an existing bucket notification rule.

### Before you begin

Before you begin, make sure that you have the following prerequisites in place:

- You have the updated event types or destination information.

### Procedure

1. Open the
Notifications
tab.
2. Select the notification and click
Edit
.
3. Update the required fields.
4. Click
Save
.
5. Verify the updates in the table.

### Results

The bucket notification is updated with the new configuration..
