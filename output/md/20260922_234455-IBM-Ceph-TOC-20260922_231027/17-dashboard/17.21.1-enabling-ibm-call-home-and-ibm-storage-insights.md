## Enabling IBM Call Home and IBM Storage Insights

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

If you have disabled Call Home, use the
IBM Storage Ceph
dashboard to enable the feature again.

### About this task

Call Home is enabled by default. If you have disabled Call Home and want to enable it again, use the following procedure. When re-enabling, all communication of data with IBM support systems is re-established.

> 📝 **Note:** Important:
Service events are not enabled by default. To enable service events for automatic support case creation, see
Enabling service events
.

### Procedure

1. Enable IBM Call Home in one of the following ways:

- Click Settings
>
IBM Call Home.
- From the banner on the Ceph dashboard, click Activate. This banner appears once every 90 days, if not yet activated.

The Configure IBM Call Home form opens.

  - Click Settings
  >
  IBM Call Home.
  - From the banner on the Ceph dashboard, click Activate. This banner appears once every 90 days, if not yet activated.

  The Configure IBM Call Home form opens.
2. Fill in the
Configure IBM Call Home
form.
Fill in the business contact information.

> 📝 **Note:** Important:
All contact information should be the business contact information for the IBM business customer. This includes names, emails, and phone numbers.
3. Click
Configure Call Home
.
The
Activated IBM Call Home Agent
notification is displayed.
4. **Optional:**
Configure the connection to IBM Storage Insights.

1. Enable IBM Storage Insights in one of the following ways:

- From the banner on the Ceph dashboard, click Opt-in.

This banner appears once every 90 days, if not yet enabled.
- Click Settings
>
IBM Storage Insights.

The Configure IBM Storage Insights form opens.

  - From the banner on the Ceph dashboard, click Opt-in.

  This banner appears once every 90 days, if not yet enabled.
  - Click Settings
  >
  IBM Storage Insights.

  The Configure IBM Storage Insights form opens.
2. Fill in the
Configure IBM Storage Insights
form.
Fill in the business contact information.
3. Click
Opt-in
.

  1. Enable IBM Storage Insights in one of the following ways:

  - From the banner on the Ceph dashboard, click Opt-in.

  This banner appears once every 90 days, if not yet enabled.
  - Click Settings
  >
  IBM Storage Insights.

  The Configure IBM Storage Insights form opens.

    - From the banner on the Ceph dashboard, click Opt-in.

    This banner appears once every 90 days, if not yet enabled.
    - Click Settings
    >
    IBM Storage Insights.

    The Configure IBM Storage Insights form opens.
  2. Fill in the
  Configure IBM Storage Insights
  form.
  Fill in the business contact information.
  3. Click
  Opt-in
  .
5. Verify the Call Home configuration from the dashboard.

1. From the navigation panel, click
Cluster
>
Manager Modules
.
2. Verify that
`call_home_agent`
is enabled.

  1. From the navigation panel, click
  Cluster
  >
  Manager Modules
  .
  2. Verify that
  `call_home_agent`
  is enabled.
