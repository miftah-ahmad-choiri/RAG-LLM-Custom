## Removing HAProxy from an NFS configuration

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Learn to remove a HAProxy from an NFS configuration by using the command-line.

To remove a HAProxy from an NFS configuration, follow the steps in the procedure in section Deleting the NFS service cluster.

To troubleshoot error related to HAProxy for NFS, collect the tcpdump from client side during failover. Also collect the NFS debug logs.
