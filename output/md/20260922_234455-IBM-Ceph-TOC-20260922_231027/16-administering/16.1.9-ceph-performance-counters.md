## Ceph performance counters

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can gather performance metrics of the IBM Storage Ceph cluster. The Ceph performance counters are a collection of internal infrastructure metrics. The collection, aggregation, and graphing of this metric data can be done by an assortment of tools and can be useful for performance analytics.
