## Configuring SNMP traps

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can deploy and configure the simple network management
protocol (SNMP) gateway in an IBM Storage Ceph cluster to receive
alerts from the Prometheus Alertmanager and route them as SNMP traps to the cluster.
