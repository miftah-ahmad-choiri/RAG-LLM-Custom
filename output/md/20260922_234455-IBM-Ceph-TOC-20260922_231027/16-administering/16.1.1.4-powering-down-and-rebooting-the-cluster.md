## Powering down and rebooting the cluster

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Use either the Ceph Orchestrator or `systemctl` commands to power down and restart the IBM Storage Ceph cluster.

Before you begin powering down IBM Storage Ceph, be sure that you have root-level access.
