## High availability

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

In addition to the high scalability enabled by the CRUSH algorithm, Ceph must also
maintain high availability.

Maintaining high availability  means that Ceph clients must be able to read and write data even
when the cluster is in a degraded state, or when a monitor fails.
