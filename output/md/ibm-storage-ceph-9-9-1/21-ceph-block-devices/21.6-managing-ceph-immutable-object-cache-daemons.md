## Managing ceph-immutable-object-cache daemons

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, use the `ceph-immutable-object-cache` daemons
to cache the parent image content on the local disk. This cache is in the local caching directory.
Future reads on that data use the local cache.
