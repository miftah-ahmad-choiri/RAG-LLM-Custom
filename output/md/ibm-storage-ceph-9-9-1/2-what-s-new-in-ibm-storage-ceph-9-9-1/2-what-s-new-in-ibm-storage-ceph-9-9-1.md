> 📝 **Note:** New versioning scheme:

IBM Storage Ceph introduces IBM’s V.R.M.F versioning scheme to the product. The four-field versioning adds a leading version number (V, standing for version), to be increased to mark major product changes. The trailing three fields (R.M.F, for release, modification, and fix) follow our previous convention, mapping to an upstream major, and subsequent minor and fix levels.

With this change, the product moves to VRMF, and consequently release 9.1 is versioned 9.9.1.0. Customers already familiar with our previous versioning model should expect no changes in the the technical meaning of R.M.F, the supported upgrade paths, or compatibility implications.

For more information, see [Why did the IBM Storage Ceph versioning model change?](https://www.ibm.com/support/pages/node/7258375) on [IBM Support](https://www.ibm.com/mysupport/).

### September 2026

Documented new known issue for IBM Storage Ceph 9.9.1.1 and later versions.

- Upgrades to IBM Storage Ceph 9.9.1.1 and later might take substantially longer than previous releases because `cephadm` rotates service authentication keys as part of the migration to the `aes256k` CephX encryption scheme. Plan maintenance windows accordingly. For more information, see Known issues in Release notes for 9.9.1.1.
