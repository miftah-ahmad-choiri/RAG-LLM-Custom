## cephadm troubleshooting

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can troubleshoot the IBM Storage Ceph cluster. Sometimes there is a need to investigate why a `cephadm` command failed or why a specific service does not run properly.

Before troubleshooting `cephadm`, be sure that you have a running IBM Storage Ceph cluster
