## Snapset metadata options

Last Updated
: 2026-09-06

Use the `ceph-objectstore-tool` utility to modify snapset metadata for troubleshooting and recovery operations.

> 📝 **Note:** Important:
The
`ceph-objectstore-tool`
utility is a low-level troubleshooting and recovery tool. Incorrect use can cause unrecoverable data loss. Perform these operations only under
[IBM Support](https://www.ibm.com/mysupport/)
guidance.

### clear-snapset command

Use the `clear-snapset` command to clear or modify snapset metadata associated with an object. These operations are intended for advanced troubleshooting scenarios.

1. Before running `ceph-objectstore-tool`, verify that the appropriate OSD is down and log into the OSD container.

1. Verify that the OSD is down.

```plaintext-ibm
systemctl status ceph-osd@OSD_ID
```

Copy to clipboard
2. Log in to the OSD container.

```plaintext-ibm
cephadm shell --name osd.OSD_ID
```

Copy to clipboard

### clear-snapset options

Get to know all of the `clear-snapset` command options for `ceph-objectstore-tool`.

`clear-snapset`
By default, clears
`clones`
,
`clone_size`
, and
`clone_overlap`
.
clear-snapset corrupt
Clear the entire snapset.
clear-snapset seq
Clear
`snapset.seq`
.
clear-snapset snaps
Clear
`clone_snaps`
.
clear-snapset clones
Clear
`clones`
.
clear-snapset clone_size
Clear
`clone_size`
.
clear-snapset clone_overlap
Clear
`clone_overlap`
.
clear-snapset size
Break all clone sizes by adding 1.

### Example

The following example clears the entire snapset for an object:

```
ceph-objectstore-tool --data-path /var/lib/ceph/osd/ceph-0 'OBJECT-JSON' clear-snapset corrupt
```
