## Trim usage

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

With heavy use, usage logs can begin to take up storage space. You can trim usage logs
for all users and for specific users. You may also specify date ranges for trim
operations.

Example

```plaintext-ibm
[ceph: root@host01 /]# radosgw-admin usage trim --start-date=2022-06-01
                    --end-date=2022-07-31

[ceph: root@host01 /]# radosgw-admin usage trim --uid=johndoe
[ceph: root@host01 /]# radosgw-admin usage trim --uid=johndoe --end-date=2021-04-31
```
