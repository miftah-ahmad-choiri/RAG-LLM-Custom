## Managing rate-limiting for NFS clusters

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Learn to manage the rate-limiting or quality of service (QoS) for NFS clusters.

There are 2 types of rate-limiting for NFS clusters:

- Bandwidth rate-limiting.
- Input/output (IOPS) rate-limiting.

You can enable and disable the rate-limiting feature by using the command line.
