## Managing mirroring functions

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can manage and monitor mirroring functions of the block
devices on the IBM Storage Ceph Dashboard.

You can add another layer of redundancy to Ceph Block Devices by mirroring data images between
storage clusters. Understanding and using Ceph Block Device mirroring can provide you protection
against data loss, such as a site failure. There are two configurations for mirroring Ceph block
devices, one-way mirroring or two-way mirroring, and you can configure mirroring on pools and
individual images.
