## Disabling Call Home

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Call Home is enabled by default. Use the
IBM Storage Ceph
dashboard to disable the feature if required.

### Procedure

Go to
Settings
>
IBM Call Home
, and click
Deactivate
. Deactivating IBM Call Home also opts you out from IBM Insights.
