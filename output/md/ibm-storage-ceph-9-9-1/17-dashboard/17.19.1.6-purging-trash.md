## Purging trash

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Purge trash using the IBM Storage Ceph
dashboard.

### Before you begin

Before you begin, make sure that you have the following prerequisites in place:

- A running IBM Storage Ceph cluster.
- Dashboard is installed.
- A pool with the `rbd` application enabled is created.
- An image is trashed.

### Procedure

1. From the dashboard navigation, go to
Block
>
Images
.
2. From the
Trash
tab, click
Purge
Trash
.
3. In the
Purge Trash
dialog, select the pool and click
Purge Trash
.A notification displays that the pools in the trash
were purged successfully.
