## Managing Ceph services

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can manage Ceph daemons on the IBM Storage Ceph Dashboard.

> 📝 **Note:** Note:
In the Ceph Dashboard, daemon processes are referred to as services. While this documentation may reference daemons when describing underlying behavior, all management actions in the dashboard are performed on services.
