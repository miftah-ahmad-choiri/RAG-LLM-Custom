## Downloading reports

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Download reports that are sent to IBM Call Home. Reports that are available to download
are inventory, status, and last contact.

### Procedure

To download reports go to
Settings
>
IBM Call
Home
, and click the download icon next to the report that you want to
download.
