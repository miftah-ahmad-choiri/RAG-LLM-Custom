## Modifying Call Home configuration

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Modify Call Home configuration from the IBM Storage Ceph dashboard.

### Procedure

1. From the navigation panel go to
Administration
>
Manager
Modules
.
2. Select
call_home_agent
.
3. Click
Edit
.
4. Change the configuration parameters for Call Home and click
Update
.
