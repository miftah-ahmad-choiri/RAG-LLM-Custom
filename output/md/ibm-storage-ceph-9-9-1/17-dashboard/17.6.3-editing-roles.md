## Editing roles

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

The dashboard allows you to edit roles on the dashboard.

### Prerequisites

- A running IBM Storage Ceph cluster.
- Dashboard is installed.
- Admin-level of access to the Dashboard.
- A role is created on the dashboard.

### Procedure

1. Log in to the Dashboard.
2. Click the Dashboard Settings icon and then click User
management.
3. On Roles tab, select the role to edit and click
Edit.
4. In the Edit Role form, edit the parameters, and then click
Edit Role.
A notification displays that the role was updated
successfully.
