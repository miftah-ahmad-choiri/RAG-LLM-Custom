## Managing roles

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can create, edit, clone, and delete roles on the
dashboard.

By default, there are eight system roles. You can create custom roles and give permissions to
those roles. These roles can be assigned to users based on the requirements.
