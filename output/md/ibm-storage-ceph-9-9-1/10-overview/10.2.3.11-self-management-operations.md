## Self management operations

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Ceph clusters perform a lot of self monitoring and management operations
automatically.

Ceph OSDs can, for example, check the cluster health and report back to the Ceph monitors. By
using CRUSH to assign objects to placement groups and placement groups to a set of OSDs, Ceph OSDs
can use the CRUSH algorithm to rebalance the cluster or recover from OSD failures dynamically.
