## Reporting

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

IBM Storage Ceph provides system monitoring and
reporting. System administrators can retrieve configuration settings at runtime.

IBM Storage Ceph provides basic system monitoring and reporting
with the `ceph-mgr` daemon plug-ins, namely, the RESTful API, the dashboard, and
other plug-ins such as `Prometheus` and `Zabbix`. Ceph collects this
information using `collectd` and sockets to retrieve settings, configuration details,
and statistical information.

In addition to default system behavior, system administrators may configure
`collectd` to report on security matters, such as configuring the
`IP-Tables` or `ConnTrack` plug-ins to track open ports and
connections respectively.
