## cephadm-ansible modules

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

The `cephadm-ansible` modules are a collection of modules that simplify
writing Ansible playbooks by providing a wrapper around `cephadm` and `ceph
orch` commands. You can use the modules to write your own unique Ansible playbooks to
administer your cluster using one or more of the modules.

The `cephadm-ansible` package includes the following modules:

- `cephadm_bootstrap`
- `ceph_orch_host`
- `ceph_config`
- `ceph_orch_apply`
- `ceph_orch_daemon`
- `cephadm_registry_login`
