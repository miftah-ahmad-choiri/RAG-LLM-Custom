## Understanding process management

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can manipulate the various Ceph daemons by type or instance in an IBM Storage Ceph cluster. Manipulating these daemons allows you to start, stop, and restart all of the Ceph services as needed.
