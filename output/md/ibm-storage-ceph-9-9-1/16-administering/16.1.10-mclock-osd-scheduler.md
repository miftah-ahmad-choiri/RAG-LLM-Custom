## mClock OSD scheduler

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

As a storage administrator, you can implement the IBM Storage Ceph's quality of service (QoS) using mClock queueing
scheduler. This is based on an adaptation of the mClock algorithm called dmClock.

The mClock OSD scheduler provides the desired QoS using configuration profiles to allocate proper
reservation, weight, and limit tags to the service types.

The mClock OSD scheduler performs the QoS calculations for the different device types, that is
SSD or HDD, by using the OSD’s IOPS capability (determined automatically) and maximum sequential
bandwidth capability (See `osd_mclock_max_sequential_bandwidth_hdd` and
`osd_mclock_max_sequential_bandwidth_ssd` in the mclock configuration options.
