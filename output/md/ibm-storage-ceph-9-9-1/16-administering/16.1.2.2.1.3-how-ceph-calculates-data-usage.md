## How Ceph calculates data usage

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

The `used` value reflects the *actual* amount of raw storage used.
The `xxx GB / xxx GB` value means the amount available, the lesser of the two
numbers, of the overall storage capacity of the cluster.

The notional number reflects the size of the stored data before it is replicated, cloned or
snapshotted. Therefore, the amount of data actually stored typically exceeds the notional amount
stored, because Ceph creates replicas of the data and may also use storage capacity for cloning and
snapshotting.
