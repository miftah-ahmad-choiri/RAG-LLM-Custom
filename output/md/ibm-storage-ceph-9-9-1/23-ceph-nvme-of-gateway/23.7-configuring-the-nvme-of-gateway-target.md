## Configuring the NVMe-oF gateway target

Last Updated
: 2026-06-25

Use `ceph nvmeof` commands to configure targets, LUNs, and clients.

Before configuring the NVMe-oF gateway target, verify that the following services are installed and running on the storage system:

- IBM Storage Ceph 9.9.0 or later cluster
- Ceph NVMe-oF gateway
- IBM Storage Ceph Dashboard 9.9.0 or later
